# Leads and exports

> Status: specified, not implemented (plan 4); the list of an event's leads, with spam marking and deletion, exists since plan 3 (EDI-020)

## Viewing

- **LEA-001** — The dashboard lists the leads by event, and offers a "toute la carte" ("whole card") view.
- **LEA-002** — The owner can search among their leads.
- **LEA-003** — The owner can open the detail view of a lead.
- **LEA-004** — From the list or the detail view, the owner can mark a lead as spam and delete it (see ABU-003 and ABU-004).
- **LEA-005** — Deleting a lead also serves to answer a GDPR erasure request.
- **LEA-013** — The "toute la carte" ("whole card") view (LEA-001) lists in a single list the leads of all the events of the card, newest first, each with the title of its event. It is reached from the card's event list (EDI-018).
- **LEA-014** — A lead list, of an event or of the whole card, shows every lead of its scope at once, spam ones included with their label (ABU-009), with no pagination.
- **LEA-015** — The search (LEA-002) filters the list on screen, within its scope (the event or the whole card). The text typed is split into words at spaces; a lead is shown when every word is found, ignoring case and accents, in at least one of: its first name, its last name, its email, its city, its phone number (see LEA-016), or its answer to a custom field of type `text`, `long text` or `single choice`. An empty search shows the whole list; when no lead matches, the list says "Aucun contact ne correspond à ta recherche." ("No contact matches your search.").
- **LEA-016** — A search word made only of digits and of the characters `+`, `.`, `-`, `(` and `)`, with at least one digit, also matches a lead whose phone number contains its digits once its leading zeros are removed: "0612" finds "+33 6 12 34 56 78".
- **LEA-017** — In a list, a lead is titled by its first and last name; without a name, by its email, which is then not repeated on its row; without either, "Contact sans nom" ("Unnamed contact").
- **LEA-018** — The detail view (LEA-003) shows the title of the lead's event, its submission date and time, each standard field it answered, each of its custom answers with the label the field had at submission (FRM-022), those of fields deleted since included (FRM-023), the consent date and time with the exact consent text (FRM-008), and whether it is marked as spam. Dates and times are shown in the user's time zone (EVT-022).
- **LEA-019** — In the detail view, a `checkbox` answer reads "Oui" ("Yes") or "Non" ("No"), a `date` answer and the date of birth are shown as French dates, a `number` answer in French number format, and a custom field left empty reads "Non renseigné" ("Not filled in").
- **LEA-020** — When marking as spam, unmarking or deleting succeeds, from the list or from the detail view, the list shows the result at once (label changed, row removed), even if reloading the list afterwards fails. Deleting a lead closes its detail view.

## Exports

- **LEA-006** — The owner can export their leads by event or for the whole card, as CSV, XLSX or JSON.
- **LEA-007** — The CSV export is UTF-8 with a BOM, so that accented characters display correctly in Excel, and it is compatible with Notion's import.
- **LEA-008** — The XLSX export is a native XLSX file.
- **LEA-009** — The JSON export is structured and keeps the field types.
- **LEA-010** — An export contains one row per lead.
- **LEA-011** — The columns of an export are, in order: the standard fields, the custom fields (including those deleted since, see FRM-023), then the metadata (event, submission date, consent, consent text, spam).
- **LEA-012** — Leads marked as spam are excluded from exports by default, with an option to include them.
- **LEA-021** — An export is downloaded from the dashboard: from an event's lead list for the leads of that event, and from the whole-card view (LEA-013) for the leads of the card. The owner chooses the format and whether to include spam leads (LEA-012); the search (LEA-015) does not filter the export. The leads of a disabled card stay exportable (LNK-008).
- **LEA-022** — The file is named `contacts-<slug>-<event>-<date>.<ext>` for an event and `contacts-<slug>-<date>.<ext>` for the whole card: `<slug>` is the card's current slug; `<event>` is the event's title put into slug form by the rules of LNK-019, cut to 40 characters, or `evenement` when nothing is left; `<date>` is the export date, `YYYY-MM-DD`, in the user's time zone; `<ext>` is `csv`, `xlsx` or `json`.
- **LEA-023** — The rows are sorted by submission date, oldest first. The standard columns, in the order of FRM-017, are "Prénom" ("First name"), "Nom" ("Last name"), "Date de naissance" ("Date of birth"), "Email", "Téléphone" ("Phone"), then "Rue" ("Street"), "Code postal" ("Postal code"), "Ville" ("City") and "Pays" ("Country") for the address (FRM-018). A standard column is present when at least one event of the export asks for its field (FRM-019) or at least one exported lead answered it; the four address columns are present or absent together. The metadata columns are "Événement" ("Event", the event's current title), "Date d'envoi" ("Submission date"), "Date du consentement" ("Consent date"), "Texte du consentement" ("Consent text") and "Spam".
- **LEA-024** — There is one custom column per custom field (LEA-011). The events of the export are taken by start date, then by identifier; for each event, its current fields in the form's order, then the fields deleted since that still have answers, in their order in the oldest lead that has them. A column's header is the field's current label or, for a deleted field, the label recorded with its most recent answer. In a whole-card export, a field of another event with the same label, ignoring case and accents, and the same type joins the existing column instead of adding one; two fields of the same event never share a column. When two columns would have the same header, the later ones get the suffix " (2)", " (3)" and so on.
- **LEA-025** — A lead's cell in a custom column holds its answer as recorded at submission (FRM-022), written according to the type recorded with that answer; it is empty when the lead left the field empty or the field did not exist when it was submitted.
- **LEA-026** — The CSV and XLSX exports give dates and times in the user's time zone (EVT-022), which the dashboard sends with the request; an unknown time zone is replaced by Europe/Paris. The headers of the two time columns name the zone used, for example "Date d'envoi (Europe/Paris)" and "Date du consentement (Europe/Paris)". The JSON export gives instants in UTC, in RFC 3339 format, like the API (EVT-022).
- **LEA-027** — The CSV export follows RFC 4180: comma separator, double quotes around a value that needs them, CRLF line ends, header row first, UTF-8 with a BOM (LEA-007). Dates are written `YYYY-MM-DD`, dates and times `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`, numbers with a point as decimal separator, `checkbox` answers and the "Spam" column "Oui" or "Non", and the phone number in E.164 format.
- **LEA-028** — In the CSV export, a text value (header included) that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return is prefixed with an apostrophe `'`, so that a spreadsheet never runs it as a formula. The phone column and the `number` answers, which the server validated, are written unchanged.
- **LEA-029** — The XLSX export has a single sheet named "Contacts", whose header row is bold and frozen. Dates are date cells (`yyyy-mm-dd`), dates and times are date cells with a time (`yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm:ss`), `number` answers are number cells, `checkbox` answers and the "Spam" column are boolean cells, and every text is a text cell, never a formula.
- **LEA-030** — The JSON export is an object with `exported_at`, `card` (`id`, `slug`), `event` (`id`, `title`, or `null` for the whole card), `include_spam` and `leads`. Each lead has `id`, `event` (`id`, `title`), `submitted_at`, `consent_at`, `consent_text`, `spam` (a boolean), `standard` (the nine standard fields of FRM-017 and FRM-018, each `null` when unanswered, the phone in E.164, the date of birth as `YYYY-MM-DD`) and `custom` (its answers as recorded, see FRM-038: `field_id`, `label`, `kind` and `value`, a string, a number, a boolean or `null`). The keys are in English and do not depend on the interface language.
- **LEA-031** — An export without any lead (none in its scope, or only spam leads left out) is still a valid file: the header row alone (CSV, XLSX), or an empty `leads` list (JSON).
- **LEA-032** — An export response carries `Content-Disposition: attachment` with the file name of LEA-022, and `Cache-Control: no-store`, so that no cache keeps the prospects' personal data.
