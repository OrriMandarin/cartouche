# Dashboard and card editor

> Status: implemented (plans 2, 3, 4 and 6); amended by plan 7

## Cards

- **EDI-001** — The dashboard lets users list, create, edit, duplicate, disable, re-enable and delete cards.
- **EDI-002** — Deleting a card requires confirmation (see LNK-012).
- **EDI-012** — The card list shows, for each card, the name, the public link and the state: "Active", "Désactivée" ("Disabled") or "Désactivée par le support" ("Disabled by support") (see LNK-011).
- **EDI-013** — To create a card, the user enters a first name and a last name, then accepts or edits the suggested slug (see LNK-019). The card is created active, with the Classique ("Classic") template and the default theme, and opens in the editor.
- **EDI-023** — Each card of the list (EDI-012) shows a thumbnail in the card's colors (TPL-026), its name and job title, its public link with a "Copier" ("Copy") button that copies it and confirms "Lien copié" ("Link copied"), its state, a summary of its events leading to them (EDI-018), a "Modifier" ("Edit") button and a menu with "Ouvrir la page publique" ("Open the public page"), "Télécharger le QR code (SVG)" and "Télécharger le QR code (PNG)" ("Download the QR code", LNK-017), "Dupliquer" ("Duplicate"), "Désactiver" or "Réactiver" ("Disable", "Re-enable") and "Supprimer…" ("Delete…"). The summary reads "{n} en cours · {m} à venir" ("{n} in progress · {m} upcoming"), a part being left out when its number is 0, and "Aucun événement à venir" ("No upcoming event") when both are 0. A card disabled by support shows "Contacter le support" ("Contact support", ADM-021) and none of the actions LNK-036 forbids.
- **EDI-024** — The confirmation of a card deletion (EDI-002) is a dialog that names the card's slug, gives the number of its events and of its leads deleted with it, and warns that the NFC tags and QR codes pointing to its link will show "Cette carte n’est pas disponible" (LNK-034); its button reads "Supprimer définitivement" ("Delete permanently").
- **EDI-025** — With no card, the list shows "Crée ta première carte" ("Create your first card") and a single button "Créer une carte" ("Create a card"). The creation page (EDI-013) shows the public link's fixed prefix and its editable slug in a monospace font, says that the link will be written in NFC tags and QR codes, and checks the slug as the user types (LNK-020, LNK-035).
- **EDI-014** — Duplicating a card copies its content, its links, its template, its theme, its settings and its media, under a new slug chosen by the user. Neither the former slugs, nor the events, nor the leads are copied, and the copy is active. A card disabled by support cannot be duplicated (`409 card_disabled_by_support`).

## Card editor

- **EDI-003** — The editor shows a form on the left and a live preview in a phone frame on the right; on a phone, two tabs "Modifier" ("Edit") and "Aperçu" ("Preview") switch between the form and the preview.
- **EDI-004** — The preview updates on every change, without needing to save.
- **EDI-005** — A switch in the editor simulates "event in progress" in the preview.
- **EDI-006** — Changes are only published on save.
- **EDI-007** — The editor form is generated from the settings schema of the chosen template (see TPL-004).
- **EDI-008** — Strings entered in the editor are normalized to NFC before being stored.
- **EDI-015** — Saving is all-or-nothing: if any part is refused (slug taken, invalid field), nothing is modified. Each error is shown next to its field, and no input is lost.
- **EDI-016** — Leaving the editor with unsaved changes requires confirmation.
- **EDI-026** — The header crop dialog shows a fixed 4:3 frame in which the owner moves and zooms (from 100% to 300%) the image or the video, over a rule-of-thirds grid; the areas the card covers (the fade, as high as the header gradient intensity, the profile photo and the video's pause button) are drawn dotted and can be hidden ("Afficher les zones couvertes", "Show covered areas"), and a preview "Sur ta carte" ("On your card") shows the result. The whole frame area is the handle: the owner drags the media anywhere in it, or pinches it on a touch screen, and the frame's corner marks are only drawn, not grabbed; the arrow keys move the media and the + and − keys zoom; "Réinitialiser" ("Reset") returns to the default crop (MED-026) and "Appliquer" ("Apply") sends the crop (MED-030). The dialog opens by itself after the owner chooses a header file that is not 4:3, before it is uploaded.
- **EDI-034** — For a header video, the crop dialog also shows a strip of frames with a playhead and two handles that set the loop's start and end (MED-028), each with a 44-pixel touch area and moved by the arrow keys when it has the focus, with the loop's length ("Boucle de 7,5 s", "7.5 s loop"); the box "Utiliser l’image à {time} comme image d’attente" ("Use the frame at {time} as placeholder image") sets the poster time to the playhead's position, at which the server extracts the placeholder image (MED-003). When it re-frames a stored video, the dialog plays its edit copy and draws the strip's frames from it (MED-037).
- **EDI-037** — When the server refuses a header video or gives up processing it (MED-039), the media field says why: "Cette vidéo ne peut pas être lue : essaie un autre fichier MP4" ("This video cannot be read: try another MP4 file") for `video_unreadable`, "Cette vidéo est trop grande (4 096 pixels de côté au plus)" ("This video is too large: 4,096 pixels per side at most") for `video_too_large`, "La préparation de la vidéo a pris trop de temps : réessaie dans un instant" ("Preparing the video took too long: try again in a moment") for `video_processing_timeout`; a file heavier than the limits says "Ce fichier est trop lourd (10 Mo pour une image, 50 Mo pour une vidéo)" ("This file is too heavy: 10 MB for an image, 50 MB for a video", MED-002, MED-012).
- **EDI-035** — The profile photo crop dialog shows a square frame under a round mask, with previews at the real sizes of the card (112 pixels) and of the dashboard (36 pixels), and works like the header dialog (EDI-026); it opens by itself after the owner chooses a photo that is not square.
- **EDI-027** — The editor shows "Modifications non enregistrées" ("Unsaved changes") as soon as the draft differs from the saved card. After a refused save, a summary "Rien n’a été enregistré : {n} points à corriger." ("Nothing was saved: {n} points to fix.") links to each field in error (EDI-015). After a successful save, a short message confirms "Carte enregistrée et publiée" ("Card saved and published").
- **EDI-028** — Following a link of the dashboard while the card editor or the event editor has unsaved changes (EDI-016) opens the dialog "Quitter sans enregistrer ?" ("Leave without saving?") with "Rester" ("Stay", the default action), "Quitter sans enregistrer" ("Leave without saving") and "Enregistrer et quitter" ("Save and leave"), which leaves only once the save has succeeded. Closing or reloading the page uses the browser's own confirmation.
- **EDI-029** — In the card editor, the links are reordered by dragging their handle or, the handle having the focus, with the up and down arrow keys; each move is announced to screen readers ("{name}, position {n} sur {total}", "{name}, position {n} of {total}"). The custom fields of the event editor are reordered the same way.
- **EDI-036** — In the card editor, a link is added either from the menu "Ajouter un réseau" ("Add a network"), which lists the seven known kinds (PUB-012) and adds a row holding only the address, or with "Ajouter un lien libre" ("Add a free-form link"), which adds a row holding a label ("Intitulé") and an address ("Adresse"). A link's kind is fixed by how it was added: to change it, the owner removes the link and adds another. A link of a known kind already stored with a label keeps an editable "Intitulé" field, which stays while the owner empties it.
- **EDI-030** — The theme section of the editor shows the eight ready-made themes (TPL-009) as thumbnails, then the settings of TPL-010, the font pair (TPL-011) and the state of the contrast checks (TPL-013): when they all pass, "Lisible : contraste du texte {a}:1, des boutons {b}:1." ("Readable: text contrast {a}:1, buttons {b}:1."), {a} and {b} being checks 1 and 3 of TPL-018 with one decimal. The section's summary is the theme's name, followed by ", personnalisé" ("customized") when one of its settings was changed.
- **EDI-017** — The simulated event of EDI-005 exists only in the preview: it is never saved nor visible on the public page.

## Events and leads

- **EDI-009** — A card's page lists its events in three groups (in progress, upcoming, past) and lets users create, edit, close and delete them (see EVT-009).
- **EDI-018** — A card's event list shows, for each event, its title, its start, its end (or "Sans fin" ("No end")), its state (EVT-020) and its number of leads. It can be reached from the card list and from the card's editor.
- **EDI-019** — The event editor sets the title, the description, the start, the end, the access code (shown in clear, see EVT-010), the per-device limit, the standard fields (not asked, optional or required), the custom fields (adding, order, type, label, help text, options, required or not) and the consent text (default or custom). Each error is shown next to its field, with no input lost (see EDI-015).
- **EDI-020** — For each event, the dashboard lists its leads, from newest to oldest, with the submission date, the name, the email, the phone and the "Spam" label; from a lead's detail view, the owner marks or unmarks it as spam and deletes it (LEA-004, see ABU-003, ABU-004, ABU-009 and ABU-010). Search, the detailed view, the "toute la carte" ("whole card") view and exports come with EDI-010.
- **EDI-010** — The dashboard gives access to the leads and their exports (see LEA-001 and LEA-006).
- **EDI-021** — Leaving the event editor with unsaved changes requires confirmation, as in the card editor (EDI-016): closing or reloading the page, and following a link of the page, ask first; after a successful save, or when the changes have been undone, nothing is asked.
- **EDI-031** — The "Contacts" entry of the navigation (EDI-022) shows the leads of one card, chosen with a card selector (the first card of the list by default): the whole card or one of its events, chosen with tabs on a computer and with a menu on a phone, each with its number of leads (see LEA-001). A line sums up the scope: "{n} contacts, dont {m} marqués comme spam" ("{n} contacts, {m} of them marked as spam"). Without any lead, the page says "Aucun contact pour l’instant" ("No contact yet") with a link "Voir mes événements" ("See my events").
- **EDI-032** — A card's event list says when the card has as many events in progress as the instance allows (EVT-004, EVT-019, the limit being given to the dashboard by the API): "{max} événements en cours : c’est le maximum par carte. Termine-en un pour en démarrer un nouveau. Tu peux toujours en programmer à venir." ("{max} events in progress: the maximum per card. End one to start a new one. You can still schedule upcoming ones."). With no event, it says "Aucun événement" ("No event") with a button "Créer un événement" ("Create an event").
- **EDI-033** — The confirmation of an event deletion (EVT-024) is titled "Supprimer « {title} » ?" ("Delete "{title}"?"), says "Les {n} contacts collectés pendant cet événement seront supprimés avec lui, définitivement." ("The {n} contacts collected during this event will be deleted with it, permanently."), offers "Exporter les contacts" ("Export the contacts", LEA-006), and its button reads "Supprimer l’événement et {n} contacts" ("Delete the event and {n} contacts"), or "Supprimer l’événement" when the event has no lead.

## Interface

- **EDI-022** — On a computer, every dashboard page has a 248-pixel side bar with the logo, the navigation "Mes cartes" ("My cards"), "Contacts" (EDI-031) and "Mon compte" ("My account"), followed for administrators only by "Support" (ADM-001), and the user's name and email at the bottom; the screens of a card show a breadcrumb in a top bar. On a phone, a top bar shows a back button, the title and an action, and a tab bar at the bottom gives the same entries (three, or four for an administrator); in the card and event editors, the tab bar is replaced by a bar holding the "Enregistrer" ("Save") button, always visible. The active entry is shown in the brand color.

- **EDI-011** — The interface is in French in v1, and all its texts go through an i18n system, so that English can be added without a rewrite.
